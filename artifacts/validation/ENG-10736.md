## Acceptance Criteria Mapping

- Additive blocked taxonomy contract is implemented in `src/listen/state.rs` and persisted
  compatibly through `src/listen/store.rs`.
- Blocked transitions are mapped through shared helpers in `src/listen/mod.rs` and
  `src/listen/worker.rs`, preserving legacy `Blocked | <reason>` summaries.
- Blocked sessions are counted separately from active and completed sessions in
  `src/listen/mod.rs::tests::session_counts_split_active_blocked_and_completed`.
- CLI surfaces render generic `Blocked` for legacy payloads and category-aware labels for structured
  payloads in:
  - `tests/listen.rs::listen_sessions_list_and_inspect_keep_legacy_blocked_fallback_without_metadata`
  - `tests/listen.rs::listen_sessions_list_and_inspect_surface_structured_blocked_category`
  - `tests/listen.rs::listen_sessions_inspect_surfaces_structured_detail_fields`
- Dashboard stage labels and `Block Detail` are covered by
  `src/listen/dashboard.rs::tests::detail_panel_surfaces_blocked_stage_and_block_detail`.
- Timed-out blocked sessions now prove the new taxonomy without collapsing timeout detail in
  `tests/listen.rs::listen_worker_times_out_builtin_turns_and_records_timeout_reporting`.
- Blocked retry behavior remains unchanged in:
  - `src/listen/store.rs::tests::retry_blocked_session_resets_to_brief_ready`
  - `src/listen/store.rs::tests::retry_blocked_session_returns_false_for_non_blocked`
