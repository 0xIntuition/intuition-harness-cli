  `listen/projects/<PROJECT_KEY>/audit/`, preserves it across TTL pruning and cleanup, and keeps

  dashboard flows, preserves lifecycle-aware reconciliation decisions, and keeps resumable

  exit detail in the selected-session pane so operators can inspect incomplete lifecycles without
  falling back to raw logs.

  lifecycle metadata, keeps timeout review guidance distinct from stalled-turn handling, and logs
  structured timeout metadata including the graceful-shutdown window and final termination path.

  truncated publishing exits, deterministic dashboard surfaces, and the dedicated timeout and
  stubborn-timeout proofs listed above.
- `README.md`, `docs/agent-daemon.md`, `docs/listen-phased-execution-spec.md`, and `WORKFLOW.md`

  that keeps subprocess timeouts distinct from stalled-turn detection.
